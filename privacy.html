Privacy Policy

Codebook VI doesn't sell your data or track you for advertising. It collects only anonymous usage and crash data, and you can turn that off.

Effective November 21, 2026

What stays on your device

Your favorites, unlocked cheats, progress counts, checklist ticks and settings are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Deleting the app removes them, and More → Reset progress and favorites clears them at any time.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in and shows no ads. It doesn't use your advertising identifier and doesn't track you across other companies' apps or websites.

Usage and crash data (Google Firebase)

To find bugs and understand which features are used, the app uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous events such as which cheats are opened, whether a code was copied, and whether Couch mode, Practice or the paywall was shown, along with device model, iOS version, app version and approximate country (derived from your IP address).
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends crash reports: what the app was doing when it crashed, device model and iOS version.
  • Firebase Remote Config downloads app settings, such as whether the Map tab is shown.

Firebase uses a random installation identifier, not your name, email or Apple Account, and the app tells Firebase not to use this data for advertising. You can stop sending usage and crash data at any time in More → Privacy → Share usage and crash data. See Firebase's privacy information.

Content updates

To deliver new cheat codes, the app downloads data files from our website, hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web request, this sends your IP address and basic technical details (such as the app's network library) to the host so the file can be delivered. The request contains nothing about you or your progress. We do not receive or store this information. GitHub may keep standard server logs; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Map (Map Genie)

The Map tab shows the website of Map Genie (mapgenie.io), an independent company, inside the app. When you open it, your device connects directly to Map Genie and to the services its website uses, which may include advertising and analytics, under Map Genie's own policies. Codebook VI doesn't send Map Genie any information about you, and the app doesn't keep Map Genie's cookies or site data after you close it.

Purchases

Codebook VI Pro is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple Account. See Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and its effective date.

Codebook VI Support

Help with the app, purchases and cheat codes.

Contact

Email benik.saratikyan@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (More → About).

A code doesn't work

Cheats only work after you finish the main story, and each cheat must be unlocked in the game first. Then open your phone with Up on the D-pad, hold L1 (LB on Xbox), press R1 (RB), and enter the directions. Codes marked Unverified may not work yet. If a Confirmed code fails after all of that, email us the cheat's name.

New codes

New and corrected codes are added to the app automatically while you're online, so there's no need to update the app. They show a NEW badge.

Codebook VI Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase that unlocks Couch mode, Practice mode, progress counters and collectible checklists. All codes, guides and tips stay free.

  • Restore on a new device: More → Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple Account.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your progress, checklists and favorites are stored only on your device. There are no accounts, ads or tracking. See the privacy policy.
